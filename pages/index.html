02 / Quotas & notifications

A heads-up.
Before the limit.

See observed quota and reset windows for Codex and Antigravity. Get a warning below 15%, a critical alert below 5%, and a reset notification when fresh observations confirm replenishment.

Session alerts can tell you when attention is needed or work finishes. Finished alerts default to at least five minutes of working time observed by Loom.

Availability depends on each provider's local interface. Claude Code does not expose quota locally.

03 / Local storage

See what adds up.

Get a clear breakdown of local agent storage, without digging through hidden folders. Configure local scans in Settings and see the details in Loom's Storage view.

Local by design

Your work.
Your Mac.

No Loom account. No hosted backend. Your agents stay in your hands.

Loom observes local processes and provider information. It does not send prompts, approve commands, or modify your projects. Hide project paths in Settings to mask them in the interface and notifications.

Antigravity quota access uses a local loopback connection and an ephemeral in-memory token.
