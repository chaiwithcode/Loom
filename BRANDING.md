# Loom branding

The Loom name and three-strand weave identify the project maintained by Deepak Sharma. Three independent strands meet in one circular woven base: **Three agents. One Loom.** The full-color icon and monochrome menu glyph express the same geometry at different sizes.

Included code, documentation and icon assets are covered by the [MIT License](LICENSE). This document adds no restrictions to those permissions and does not reserve the artwork under a separate license.

Clearly identify a fork's maintainer and changes so people can distinguish it from the official project. A distinct name or icon is encouraged when the fork has a different identity. Do not imply endorsement, affiliation or official status without permission. No registered trademark status is asserted here.

References and screenshots may credit Loom by name. Provider names identify integrations; the mark contains no vendor logos and claims no vendor endorsement. See [icon design notes](docs/ICON_DESIGN.md).
